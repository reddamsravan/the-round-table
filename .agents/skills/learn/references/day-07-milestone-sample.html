Audit Prompt 1: First Principles vs Analogy (Day 1)

Explain how first principles thinking differs from copying others.

Mastery Standard: Analogy copies past forms with small tweaks. First principles reduces problems to bare facts and builds up from physics or logic.

Audit Prompt 2: Spotting Confirmation Bias (Day 3)

How do you test your own plan for bias?

Mastery Standard: Actively search for evidence that disproves your pet idea. If you only gather supporting data, you create blind spots.

Audit Prompt 3: Second-Order Effects (Day 5)

What question uncovers ripple effects?

Mastery Standard: Always ask what happens next. Trace ripples through time past quick first gains.
